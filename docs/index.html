Job Radar

GTM · BD · Partnerships · Strategy roles open to an India-based candidate. Updated daily.

How roles get on this list
  • Remote · Worldwide: the location says anywhere/worldwide/global, or the ad says you can work from anywhere, with no "must be based in…" line.
  • Remote · APAC/India: remote roles open to India or APAC. Roles restricted to India only, which almost always come from Indian companies, are left out.
  • Visa sponsorship / relocation: roles in Europe, the UK, Australia or NZ where the ad itself mentions sponsorship, relocation or a Blue Card. The quoted evidence is shown on each card.
  • Licensed UK / NL sponsor: the ad says nothing about visas, but the company is on the UK Home Office or Dutch IND sponsor register. Ask the recruiter before applying.
  • Your non-negotiables, applied to every role:
    1. Role type: GTM, Business Development, Partnerships, Strategy, Chief of Staff / Founder's Office.
    2. Experience: you have ~4+ years. Any ad asking for more than 5 years minimum is removed. Director, Principal, VP and C-level titles are removed. "Head of" is kept only if the ad asks for 5 years or less. Intern and junior titles are removed.
    3. Location: remote-worldwide, remote open to India/APAC, or a sponsored role in Europe, UK, Australia or New Zealand. Remote roles limited to the US, UK, EU or EMEA are removed.
    4. Visas: any ad saying "no sponsorship" or "must already have the right to work" is removed.
    5. Company: startups/scale-ups; no India-HQ startups.
    6. Freshness: posted within the last 30 days; refreshed daily at 06:00 IST.
  • Sources: company career boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee, Personio, SmartRecruiters), Himalayas, RemoteOK, Remotive, Jobicy, Working Nomads and We Work Remotely.
